Click Paste Support

How can we help?

Click Paste is a local clipboard panel keyboard for iPhone and iPad.

Set up the keyboard

  1. Open Click Paste and review the privacy explanation.
  2. Give explicit clipboard consent in the app.
  3. Add Click Paste in iOS Keyboard settings.
  4. Enable Full Access to make local clipboard history available.

Clipboard and image behavior

The keyboard reads supported clipboard content only while it is visible and active. Text input is explicit.

Manage local data

Use the main app to preview, pin, archive, delete, or clear locally cached items and to revoke clipboard consent.

Accessibility and feedback

Click Paste supports system accessibility settings. Include your device model, iOS version, and the screen or action involved when reporting a problem. Never email sensitive clipboard content.
