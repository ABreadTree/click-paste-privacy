Click Paste

Privacy Policy

Your clipboard stays on your device. No analytics, no uploads, and no advertising.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Local by design. Click Paste is an iOS app and custom Keyboard Extension for reusing copied text and images. It does not require an account and is not a background clipboard monitor or cloud clipboard service.

Consent and clipboard access

Clipboard history is off until you give explicit consent in the Click Paste app. Automatic capture requires consent, Auto-cache, Full Access, and a visible, active keyboard.

The keyboard does not read or poll the clipboard while hidden, inactive, or in the background. URL-like strings are treated as ordinary text.

Local storage and retention

Clipboard content stays in the app's on-device App Group container. SQLite stores metadata, while local files store images, thumbnails, and large content bodies.

  • Up to 50 recent active items by default.
  • Unpinned items expire after 7 days; pinned items do not expire under the default rule.
  • Images share a 200 MB local storage budget.

Why Full Access is requested

Full Access lets the keyboard read the pasteboard while it is visible and active and share the local cache with the containing app. Without Full Access, the keyboard remains a setup/status panel and clipboard history is unavailable.

Your controls

You can revoke consent at any time. Close preview does not delete content; selected-item deletion removes that cached item.

Archive local cache hides active entries by marking them archived. Confirmed Clear local cache permanently deletes active and archived metadata and blob files from the device.

Collection, sharing, and tracking

Click Paste does not upload clipboard text, URL-like strings, images, previews, typed keys, input context, or settings. It contains no ads or third-party analytics SDKs and declares no tracking or collected data.

The only declared required-reason API is App Group UserDefaults with reason 1C8F.1.

Image handling

Image insertion is best effort. Some host apps do not accept images from custom keyboards; Click Paste provides a copy fallback and does not promise universal image insertion.

This website

This page adds no analytics, advertising, cookie-setting code, forms, remote fonts, or client-side scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log IP addresses and request metadata for security and service operation under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

Material changes to Click Paste data behavior will be reflected here and in applicable App Store privacy disclosures before release. The date at the top identifies the current revision.

Contact

For a privacy question, open an issue in the public privacy repository. Do not include clipboard contents, typed text, images, or other sensitive information.
